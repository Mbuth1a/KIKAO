LOOK OUT FOR YOUR CREW

Safety is
part of the plan.

Practical controls for meeting new people and travelling together.

⌖

Meet in public

Choose a licensed public venue or agreed public pickup point. Avoid moving to a private location with people you have just met.

◉

Verify the crew

Review profile verification and host details. Never share your PIN, passwords or unlocked phone.

↗

Share your plan

Send your plan, host profile and check-in time to a trusted contact before leaving.

◎

Drink safety

Keep your drink in sight, accept drinks only from trusted sources, and leave with your own trusted group if you feel unsafe.

⌁

Road-trip rule

A designated driver must remain alcohol-free. Agree on pickup, route, return time and cost split before departure.

!

Need urgent help?

If you are in immediate danger, move to a safe public place and contact local emergency services.

Report, block and get help.

Every plan and profile has a report option. Kikao's demo controls do not contact emergency services or provide live monitoring.
